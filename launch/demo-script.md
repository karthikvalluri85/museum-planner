# Demo script: 30-second screen recording

One museum (the Met). Three browser windows side by side, each about 400 px wide so the phone layout shows. No voiceover needed; captions carry it. Record at 1080p.

## Before recording

- Open the deployed URL in all three windows and pick the Met in each.
- Pre-fill each form so the recording starts on a tap of "Build my plan":
  - **Left, parent:** interests "animals" and "arms and armor"; 45 minutes; "With a young child" on.
  - **Middle, wheelchair user:** interests "egyptian art" and "paintings"; 90 minutes; "Step-free" on.
  - **Right, lunch break:** no interests; 30 minutes; "Short visit" on.
- Use interests that appear as chips for the Met on the day; swap in whichever chips exist.
- Run each once beforehand so no window hits a cold start on camera.
- Check the middle plan shows the "Step-free route" banner. If it shows "no step-free route" instead, that is the product working; pick interests on a floor the graph can reach, or record it as is and change the caption.

| 0:24 to 0:28 | Back to the left window. Tap "I'm here" on stop 2, tap "Running late", choose 15 minutes. The remaining stops are replaced. | Running late? One tap replans the rest from where you are. |
| 0:28 to 0:30 | Hold on the footer notice. | Built from public museum data. Museum plans for the rest of us. |

## Do not

- Do not say or show the word the kickoff brief bans (the one Smartify uses to describe its tours).
- Do not crop out the footer notice or the floor-data notice.
- Do not claim the step-free route is verified. The app says the floor data is hand-built; leave that visible.
